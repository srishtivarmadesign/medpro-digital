THE SYSTEM

Here's what that system looks like in practice

01

Attract the right people

We set up and manage your Meta ad campaigns using creatives and targeting built specifically for aesthetic and wellness clinics. No generic ad strategies. Every element is designed to attract patients who can pay and are ready to decide.

02

Filter intent before they enter your pipeline

Our lead forms are built to qualify leads before they reach your team. Questions that filter curiosity from intent — so your tele caller only speaks to people who are genuinely interested in a consultation.

03

Instant automated follow-up

The moment a lead submits a form, an automated WhatsApp sequence goes live. Personalised, clinic-branded messages that respond in seconds — not hours. The window to capture a patient's attention is short. We don't miss it.

04

Nurture until they're ready

Not every lead books on day one. Our nurture sequences stay in contact over days and weeks — providing relevant information, addressing common objections, and keeping your clinic top of mind until they're ready to book.

05

Your team closes

When a lead is warm, your telecaller gets the signal. We provide a structured script, objection-handling SOPs, and a CRM view that shows everything they need to convert the call into a confirmed consultation.
